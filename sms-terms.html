SMS terms

Effective October 10, 2026. Program: Subway Hermitage Towne Plaza order texts, operated by SaibabaKrupa Inc., the independent franchise owner of Subway® #6722, 2427 E State St, Hermitage, PA 16148.

How you opt in

You opt in verbally during a phone call to the store. Before sending anything, the store's phone assistant (or a staff member) asks, for example, "Is it OK if I text you the ordering links?" or "Can I text you a confirmation?" We send a text only after you say yes, and only to the number you are calling from or the number you give us. The confirmation text we send is the record of your opt-in. We never send texts to people who did not ask for them, and opting in is not required to place an order.

What we send

  • Online-ordering links (for example the Subway app and DoorDash links for this store) when you ask to order ahead.
  • Catering order confirmations: a summary of your catering or large order, including any delivery fee and how payment will be collected.
  • Catering order notices: follow-ups about that order, for example when the store needs to confirm details.

These are transactional messages about your own call or order. We do not send marketing or promotional texts under this program.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Messages are transactional, usually one or two per call or order.

Cost

Msg & data rates may apply. Check with your wireless carrier for details of your plan.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to any message to opt out. We will send one confirmation and then no further texts. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call (724) 983-1088 or email ketanrpatel@yahoo.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
